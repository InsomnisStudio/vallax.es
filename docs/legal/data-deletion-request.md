If you want to request the deletion of your personal data associated with Vallax, you can contact us directly at:

- **Contact email:** [vallaxdev@gmail.com](mailto:vallaxdev@gmail.com)

To help us process the request securely, please include:

- The email address associated with your account or previous contact with Vallax.
- The reason for the request, if you want to add context.
- Any details that help us correctly identify your information.

Once we receive your request, we will review it and reply as soon as possible. When applicable, we will delete personal data that is no longer necessary, except for any information we must keep to comply with legal obligations.

If you have any privacy-related questions, you can use the same email address.
