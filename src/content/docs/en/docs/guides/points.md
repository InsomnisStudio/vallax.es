**Points** are Vallax's in-app currency, redeemed in the **points shop**. Everything is in the **Invite and earn** section of the app.

Points **are not AI tokens**: they are different things, although you can also get tokens with points.

## How to earn points

You earn **1 point for every friend** who uses your code and creates their first search.

1. In **Invite and earn → Invite** you will find **your code**. Copy it or tap **Share code**.
2. Your friend downloads Vallax and creates an account.
3. In the app, your friend goes to **Invite and earn → Earn** and enters your code. They must do it within their **first 2 days** after creating the account.
4. When your friend creates their **first search**, you get **1 point** and a notification.

> **Important:** the invitation link only leads to the app download. **It does not count on its own**: your friend must enter the code in **Invite and earn → Earn**.

### Conditions

- It must be a **new account** from someone who has never had a Vallax account, including from the same phone.
- You cannot invite yourself or use the code from a phone where the inviter's account was already used.
- You can earn at most **20 points from invitations per month**. Friends beyond that limit do not give a point.
- Each friend gives a point **only once**, even if they later delete the search.

## Points shop

| What you get | Cost |
|---|---|
| 7 days of Plus | 3 points |
| 30 days of Plus | 10 points |
| 7 days of Pro (no AI tokens) | 7 points |
| 30 days of Pro + 25,000 AI tokens | 25 points |
| 10,000 AI tokens | 15 points |

With just **3 friends** you can already redeem a week of Plus.

### Redemption rules

- **7 days of Pro do not include tokens.** You get every Pro feature, but to use AI you need tokens from another source.
- The **25,000 tokens** from 30 days of Pro expire when those days end.
- The **10,000 tokens** from the shop work like a pack: they never expire.
- You cannot redeem days of a plan **equal to or lower than the one you already pay for**. For example, if you subscribe to Pro you cannot redeem days of Plus. If you pay for Plus, you can redeem days of Pro.
- Plan days cannot be redeemed during the Pro **free trial**.
- Tokens can always be redeemed.
- If you redeem days of Plus while Pro is active, the Plus days start counting when Pro ends. You lose nothing.
- If you redeem several times, the days add up.

We notify you before redeemed days run out. When they end, your plan goes down and the rules in [If you downgrade](/en/docs/guides/plans#if-you-downgrade) apply.

## Activity

In **Invite and earn** you can see your balance, how many friends you have invited and the **activity** history: redeemed codes, shop redemptions and points received.
