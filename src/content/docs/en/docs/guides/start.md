**Vallax** is an app for **iPhone and Android** that notifies you when a new listing appears that matches what you are looking for.

Instead of checking several platforms over and over, you create a **search** with your filters and Vallax watches for you, 24 hours a day.

> In the app, alerts are called **searches**. In these docs both words mean the same thing.

## How it works

Vallax checks **new listings**. It does not search through old ones.

The idea is not to help you browse listings that are already published, but to notify you as soon as something that matches your search goes live. If a valid listing appears, you get a notification right away.

Every notification is saved in the app, so you can review them later, filter them by platform and save the ones you like in **Favourites**.

## Supported platforms

Vallax currently works with:

- Wallapop
- Vinted
- Milanuncios
- Todocoleccion

On Vinted you can choose your country's domain from your **profile**.

## What else you can do

- **Test a search before saving it.** Vallax shows you the listings from the last 24 hours that would have matched and how many notifications per day you would get.
- **Filter with AI.** Add a plain-language instruction and the AI checks every listing before notifying you. [Learn more](/en/docs/guides/first-alert#ai-filter).
- **Find out what something sells for.** From a notification, the [How much is it?](/en/docs/guides/price-check) button looks for similar listings and gives you a reference price.
- **Share searches.** Generate a code so someone else can import your search, or import theirs.
- **Earn points by inviting friends** and redeem them for days of Plus or Pro. [See how](/en/docs/guides/points).

## Plans

Vallax has a **Free** plan and two paid plans, **Plus** and **Pro**. Every plan gets listings at the same speed: no plan is notified before another. What changes is how many searches, keywords and platforms you can use, and whether regex and AI tokens are included.

Everything is explained in [Plans and AI tokens](/en/docs/guides/plans).

## Who it is for

Vallax can be useful for many people, but it fits especially well for:

- **Resellers** who need to reach good deals before everyone else.
- **Collectors** looking for specific items who do not want to check several apps all day.
- **Buyers** who simply want a better price without wasting time scrolling and repeating searches.
