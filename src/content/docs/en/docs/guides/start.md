**Vallax** is an app for **iPhone and Android** that alerts you when a new listing appears that matches what you are looking for.

Instead of checking several marketplaces over and over again, you create an alert with your filters and Vallax watches for you.

## How it works

Vallax checks **new listings**, not old listings that were already published.

That matters: the goal is not to help you browse existing results, but to notify you the moment something new appears that fits your alert.

If a valid listing appears, you receive a notification right away.

## Supported marketplaces

Right now Vallax works with these marketplaces:

- Wallapop
- Vinted
- Milanuncios
- Todocoleccion

## Who it is for

Vallax can be useful for many people, but it fits especially well in these situations:

- **Resellers** who need to get to good opportunities before everyone else.
- **Collectors** looking for specific items without checking multiple apps all day.
- **Buyers** who simply want better prices without wasting time scrolling and repeating searches.

## What problem it solves

Searching manually across several marketplaces takes time and forces you to keep checking your phone constantly.

With Vallax, you set up an alert once and wait for the notification to arrive only when something relevant appears. That reduces noise and removes the need to keep refreshing pages yourself.
