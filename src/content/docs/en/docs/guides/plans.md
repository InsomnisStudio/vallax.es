Vallax has three plans: **Free**, **Plus** and **Pro**. You can start for free and change plan whenever you want from the **Plans** screen in the app.

**Every plan gets listings at the same speed.** Vallax checks the platforms at the same frequency for everyone: no plan is notified before another. What changes is capacity and advanced features.

## Comparison

| | Free | Plus | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | €0 | €3.99 | €11.99 |
| Annual price | €0 | €29.99 | €99.99 |
| Active searches | 1 | 5 | 50 |
| Keywords per search | 5 | 15 | 50 |
| Platforms per search | 1 | 4 | 4 |
| Regex and ignore regex | No | No | Yes |
| AI tokens included per month | 0 | 0 | 25,000 |
| Buy AI token packs | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Prices are those in Spain on the App Store and Google Play, taxes included. In other countries the store may show a different price: the final price is the one you see in the app when you subscribe.

### Which one is right for me?

- **Free:** to watch a single search on one platform.
- **Plus:** to follow several searches on every platform with more keywords.
- **Pro:** for deep searching, with many searches, regex and 25,000 AI tokens every month.

## Pro free trial

The **Pro monthly** plan may include a **7-day free trial**, managed by the App Store or Google Play.

- During the trial you get every Pro feature and **1,000 AI tokens**.
- Before it ends we notify you, so you can decide whether to continue, switch to Plus or cancel.
- If you cancel before it ends, you pay nothing.

## Subscribing, changing and cancelling

- You subscribe from the app and the **App Store or Google Play** charges you. It renews automatically until you cancel.
- To **cancel** or request a **refund**, use your store's subscription management (there is also a shortcut in **Plans → Manage subscription**).
- If you change phone or reinstall the app, use **Restore purchase** on the plans screen.
- Upgrading from Plus to Pro is immediate. If you downgrade from Pro to Plus, you usually keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

## If you downgrade

When your plan goes down (because you cancel, change plan or some days redeemed with points run out), **Vallax never deletes your searches**. Instead:

- Searches above the new limit are marked **Paused by your plan**, starting with the most recent.
- Searches that use regex, or have more keywords or platforms than allowed, are also paused until you edit them to fit.
- If you upgrade again, searches **paused by your plan resume automatically** up to the new limit.

A search you paused manually does not resume on its own.

## AI tokens

**AI tokens** are the balance used by Vallax's artificial intelligence features:

- The [AI filter](/en/docs/guides/first-alert#ai-filter) in searches.
- The [How much is it?](/en/docs/guides/price-check) button, which estimates what an item sells for.

**You do not need a paid plan to use AI.** Any plan can use it if it has tokens.

### How to get them

| Source | Amount | Expiry |
|---|---|---|
| Pro subscription (monthly or annual) | 25,000 per month | Renewed every month on your subscription date. Unused tokens do not roll over and are lost when you leave Pro |
| Pro free trial | 1,000 | When the trial ends |
| Basic pack | 10,000 for €2.99 | Never expire |
| Popular pack | 50,000 for €8.99 | Never expire |
| Pro pack | 100,000 for €14.99 | Never expire |
| Points shop | 10,000 for 15 points | Never expire |
| Points shop: 30 days of Pro | 25,000 as a gift | When those Pro days end |

On the annual Pro plan you also get 25,000 tokens **every month**, not all at once.

### Your three balances

On the **AI tokens** screen your balance is split into three:

- **Monthly:** from your Pro subscription or free trial.
- **Extra:** gifted with a points shop redemption.
- **From packs:** the ones you buy or redeem as a pack.

Vallax spends the ones that expire first (monthly or extra) and leaves pack tokens for last. **Pack tokens never expire and are never lost when you change plan.**

## Get days of Plus or Pro for free

You can get days of Plus or Pro without paying by inviting friends and redeeming points in the shop. It is explained in [Points and invitations](/en/docs/guides/points).
