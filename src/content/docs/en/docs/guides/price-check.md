You get a notification and the question is always the same: **is it a good price?** The **How much is it?** button helps you answer it in under a minute.

## How it works

1. Open a notification and tap **How much is it?**
2. **Crop the item** in the photo with your finger. If there are several things in the picture, crop only the one you care about.
3. Vallax uses AI to look for **similar listings** on Vinted and Todocoleccion. It can take up to a minute.

## What it shows you

- **Sells for about…** The reference price that item is selling for.
- **Costs you about…** What the listing would cost you, adding buyer protection and shipping. Shipping is approximate.
- **Margin.** The difference, as a percentage, between what it costs you and what you could get by reselling it.
- **Relevant listings.** The listings used to calculate the price. Check them before deciding.

If there are not enough similar listings, Vallax tells you instead of making up a price. Try cropping only the item and search again.

## AI tokens

Each check uses **AI tokens** based on its real cost, and at the end you will see how many it spent and how many you have left.

- You need some balance (more than **20 tokens**) to run a check.
- It is available on **every plan**, including Free, as long as you have tokens.
- If you already checked a notification's price using the full photo, checking it again does not spend tokens again.

How to get tokens: [Plans and AI tokens](/en/docs/guides/plans#ai-tokens).

## Keep in mind

- It is an **estimate** based on published listings, not a guaranteed price.
- It only works on notifications that still exist. If the notification was deleted and only the copy in your favourites remains, it cannot be calculated.
